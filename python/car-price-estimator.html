Why this number — market context

Average price by brand

Brand alone spans budget city cars to premium SUVs, so this shows where the selected brand sits among the others in the dataset - useful context for whether the estimate looks like a budget or premium result.

Price by engine size

Engine size turned out to be the single strongest driver of price in this dataset - more so than mileage or year - likely because bigger engines mark larger, pricier vehicle classes (MPVs, SUVs) rather than just raw power.

Price by mileage band

Higher mileage tends to mean a lower price, though the effect is weaker here than engine size or brand - this shows the average by mileage band, with your mileage marked.

What the model actually weighs

The model's own feature-importance ranking - a direct look at which inputs move the prediction most, so the estimate isn't a black box.

Model: gradient-boosted decision trees (150 trees, depth 3), trained on 609 cleaned Indonesian used-car listings (2010–2023). Held-out test set: R² 0.926, MAPE 6.1%. 5-fold cross-validation (more representative given the small dataset): R² 0.842 ± 0.059.
